What is being measured

The question is narrow on purpose. For each seller of AI infrastructure, what share of its own disclosed backlog is owed by a customer whose funding traces back to that same seller? Not "is there a bubble", not "is the backlog real". Just: how much of the promise to pay was financed by the party being paid.

The headline formula

RTE(i) = sum over customers j of D[i,j] × Provenance(i→j)

D[i,j] is the share of seller i's disclosed backlog that customer j has committed. The denominator is i's own disclosed figure, in a fixed preference order: tagged remaining performance obligations, then a quoted contracted-backlog number, then trailing revenue. Nothing is inferred.

Provenance(i→j) is how much of j's funding came from i, on three readings:

  • LOWER: dollars i demonstrably handed j, divided by what j promised to pay i, capped at 1. Direct edges only. No multi-hop.
  • CENTRAL: i's share of j's documented external funding, with funding that reaches j through intermediaries traced back and discounted one factor of alpha per hop.
  • UPPER: if i funded j at all under T1 or T2 evidence, the whole of j's commitment to i counts. This answers a different question: how much backlog sits with customers who are not funding-independent of the seller.

Why bounds instead of a number

Because the denominator is unknowable. OpenAI, Anthropic and xAI file nothing, so their total external funding is only ever a documented subset. A single point estimate of "i's share of j's funding" would silently assume the documented subset is the whole, which inflates every supplier's share. LOWER and UPPER bracket that ignorance instead of hiding it.

Evidence tiers

T1 is an SEC filing, T2 a company release or investor relations page, T3 a media report, T4 unverified. The headline admits T1 and T2 only. T3 appears in a labelled sensitivity view. T4 never enters the ledger at all; the review command refuses it. Every row carries a source link, a verbatim quote under 25 words, and an accession number if it is T1.

Binding versus not

Status is one of definitive, conditional, non-binding letter of intent, announced, or reported. Each carries an explicit weight per bound, set in configuration before any result was computed, and varied in the stress lab. A letter of intent is never counted at full weight by default.

Direction convention

Money-flow. An arrow starts at whoever pays. Funding edges run funder to recipient; purchase and capacity edges run buyer to seller. A round trip is therefore a two-cycle, which is asserted in the test suite.

Every definition above was written into DECISIONS.md before any number was computed, and is amended only by a dated entry that says why.
