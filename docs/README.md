1. [The research question](01-overview.md) explains how a buyer and seller can form a funding loop.
2. [Data and ledger](02-data-and-ledger.md) follows a filing from evidence to a reviewed row.
3. [Metrics and stress](03-metrics-and-stress.md) defines the denominator, financing bounds and shock assumptions.
4. [The deal agent](04-deal-agent.md) runs a local extraction and explains how to review a proposal.
5. [Evaluation](05-evaluation.md) explains the split, scoring and limitations.
6. [Design decisions](06-decisions.md) points to the original dated reasoning.
7. [Glossary](07-glossary.md) translates the financial and machine-learning terminology.

For a presentation, use the [interview walkthrough](WALKTHROUGH.md). The [prior agent log](history/AGENT_PROGRESS.md) and [night 3 log](history/NIGHT3.md) record what was tried.
