# What the dashboard measures

A cloud provider funds a lab. The lab signs a contract to buy capacity from that provider. The contract contributes to the seller's backlog while some of its buyer's funding comes from the same seller. This project measures how much disclosed backlog can be traced around a loop like that.

On October 5, 2026 the reviewed ledger yielded $46.7 billion on the central reading, against $2.72 trillion of disclosed backlog. Change the financing assumptions and the share goes from 1.39% to 18.22%. Only 20.7% of that backlog can be assigned to named buyers. Those two sources of uncertainty matter for different reasons. The bounds describe how financing is attributed. Missing customer names prevent attribution altogether.

The dashboard's Headline panel shows the denominator. The Graph panel lets a reader select an edge and read its evidence. The Stress Lab asks how the result changes if a customer loses funding and cuts its orders. Pending agent proposals are visible in News.
